1. Overview

MKA Labs ("we," "us," or "our") is an independent software lab that builds tools for TikTok content management and scheduling. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our services, including our integration with the TikTok API.

Short version: We access only the TikTok data necessary to provide our service. Your access tokens are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any third party for advertising or commercial purposes.

2. Data We Access from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account to MKA Labs, we may access the following data through TikTok's official API, depending on the permissions you grant:

  • Account information: Your TikTok username, display name, and profile picture (public information).
  • Video data: A list of videos you have published, including titles, descriptions, view counts, and engagement metrics.
  • Scheduling data: Post scheduling information that you create within our tool.
  • Analytics data: Aggregated engagement metrics (views, likes, shares, comments) for your content.

We access only the data required to provide the features you use. We do not access your private messages, followers list, or any data not directly related to content management and scheduling.

3. Why We Use Your Data

We use the data accessed through TikTok's API solely to provide and improve our service:

  • To display your TikTok content and analytics within our dashboard.
  • To schedule and publish posts on your behalf at the times you choose.
  • To generate insights and recommendations based on your content performance.
  • To authenticate your sessions securely using your access token.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the features of MKA Labs tools.

4. How Data Is Stored

Access tokens are stored locally on your device — in your browser's local storage or in a local configuration file, depending on how you use our tools. These tokens are never transmitted to or stored on MKA Labs servers.

Content data (such as your video list and analytics) is fetched directly from TikTok's API when you use the application and is not permanently stored on our servers. Any temporary caching that occurs is short-lived and is cleared when your session ends.

If you use a server-based version of our tools, minimal operational data (such as scheduled post metadata) may be stored on our servers solely for the purpose of executing scheduled tasks. This data is encrypted at rest and is deleted once the scheduled action is completed.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

We may share limited data in these specific circumstances only:

  • TikTok: Data is exchanged with TikTok's API as necessary to provide our service (scheduling, publishing, reading analytics). This is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or valid government request.
  • Safety: We may share data if we believe it is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of MKA Labs, our users, or the public.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics platforms, or any third party for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our service. If you unlink your TikTok account or stop using MKA Labs tools, any stored data associated with your account will be deleted within 30 days.

Access tokens stored locally on your device persist until you revoke access or clear your local storage. We have no control over or access to locally stored tokens.

7. Your Rights & Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of any data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct any inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your data from our systems.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent: Unlink your TikTok account at any time to stop further data access.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@alsaiel.com.

8. How to Unlink Your Account

You can stop MKA Labs from accessing your TikTok data at any time:

  1. Open the MKA Labs tool and go to Settings.
  2. Select Disconnect TikTok Account and confirm.
  3. Additionally, go to your TikTok account settings → Privacy → Apps and Websites → find MKA Labs and click Remove access.

After unlinking, we will no longer be able to access your TikTok data. Any locally stored tokens are cleared when you disconnect through the app.

9. Children's Privacy

MKA Labs tools are not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of MKA Labs tools after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
